Ghostline
A resurrected 1980s answering machine for seniors. Every call reaches Ghostline first.
Known contacts ring straight through. Unknown callers are screened by an AI voice that
asks who they are and why they are calling, scores the call for scam signals, challenges
anyone claiming to be family for the family safe word, can call the real family member
to confirm they are safe, and alerts the family by SMS with a dashboard link.
Users: the senior (does nothing new) and family members (set up and monitor via dashboard).
Hackathon: Kiroween 2026, Resurrection category. Theme: dead tech brought back to life.
Never promise deepfake voice detection. The safe word is the defence: a clone can copy a
voice but not a secret.
