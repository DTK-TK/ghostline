Privacy and safety rules
Every screened call announces it is screened (consent + deterrent).
Store the safe word only as a bcrypt hash. Compare with a normalised attempt.
Never log API keys, auth tokens or full phone numbers in console output.
Fraud reports are generated for the family to file; never auto-submit to police/CAFC.
Demo data uses fake names and teammates' verified numbers only.
